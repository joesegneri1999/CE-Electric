About CE Electric

A commercial electrical company serving Texas, Oklahoma, and Arkansas, with a specialty in fuel-site electrical work.

Who We Are

Commercial Electrical With a Fuel-Site Focus

CE Electric is a commercial electrical company serving Texas, Oklahoma, and Arkansas, with a specialty in fuel-site electrical work. Our services range from dispenser and pump power and tank monitoring to commercial new construction, tenant finish-outs, and electrical upgrades.

As the sister company of Carbon Environmental, CE Electric also supports coordinated fuel-site projects involving electrical, tank, and piping work.
